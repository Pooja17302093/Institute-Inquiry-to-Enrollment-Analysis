# IT Institute – Inquiries & Enrollments Dataset

Lead-to-enrollment data for an IT training institute operating across 10 Indian cities. Each row is one student inquiry, tracked through to its current status and, if the student enrolled, the enrollment date and fee paid.


## Key insights

**Headline numbers**
- 2,500 inquiries led to **935 enrollments, a 37.4% conversion rate**.
- Total fees collected: **₹2.42 crore** (₹24,217,250), averaging **₹25,901** per enrolled student.
- Pipeline status: 37.4% enrolled, 25.7% in follow-up, 25.4% not interested, 11.5% demo attended.
- Students enroll quickly: a median of **11 days** after inquiry (range 1–21 days).

**Courses**
- **Full Stack Development** is the volume and revenue leader: 486 inquiries and ₹54.9 lakh (23% of revenue).
- **Python & Data Science** is second at ₹39.5 lakh.
- **Cyber Security** punches above its size: only 231 inquiries, but ₹33.8 lakh from the highest average fee (₹34,794) and a 42% conversion rate.
- Digital Marketing, UI/UX Design, and Software Testing are the lowest-ticket courses (average fees of roughly ₹16–18K).

**Marketing channels**
- Volume is spread evenly across 8 sources, with no single channel dominating.
- **Justdial, Google Ads, and Website** convert best (39–40%) and produce the most revenue.
- **Facebook is the weakest channel**: 312 inquiries, 32% conversion, and the lowest revenue (₹24.8 lakh).

**Cities**
- **Coimbatore converts best at 45%**, well above the 37% average, and is the top revenue city despite having fewer inquiries than Delhi or Hyderabad.
- **Kolkata (32%), Hyderabad (34%), and Kochi (34%)** convert worst, which suggests room for improvement on lead quality or follow-up.

**Counselors**
- Conversion is tightly clustered (35–39%), so no counselor is an outlier.
- **Sunita R** brings in the most revenue (₹52.4 lakh). **Mohan P** has a solid 38% conversion but the lowest average fee (₹23,740).
- **Divya S** has the lowest conversion (35%) but a high average fee (₹27,275).

**Trends over time**
- Monthly inquiries are steady at about 170–220.
- Conversion by inquiry month swings between roughly 33% and 44%, with highs in Oct 2025, May 2026, and Oct 2026 and a soft patch from June to August 2026 (about 33–35%).
- Gender split is nearly even (51% male, 49% female) with identical 37% conversion.

## Takeaways

1. **Double down on high-value courses** such as Cyber Security and Full Stack, where average fees and conversion are strongest.
2. **Review Facebook spend**: it brings similar lead volume to other channels but converts worst.
3. **Replicate Coimbatore's approach** in low-converting cities like Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Kochi.
4. **Act fast on warm leads.** Most enrollments happen within three weeks, so the 642 leads in follow-up and 288 who attended a demo are time-sensitive.
5. **Look into the mid-2026 conversion dip** (June–August), which could reflect seasonality or process changes.
